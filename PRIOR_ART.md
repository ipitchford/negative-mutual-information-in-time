# Prior-art record (15 November 2026)

Question: has a violation of non-negativity of the Fullwood-Parzygnat mutual information in time (dynamical mutual
information) been published in input dimension below five, with a qubit output, or with a rank-two initial state?

Sources read in full: Fullwood & Parzygnat, Entropy 27(4) 331 (2025) (published text via PMC, and arXiv:2306.01831v2);
Fullwood & Yang, arXiv:2608.28946v1; Wu, Parzygnat, Vedral & Fullwood, arXiv:2410.02137v1 (New J. Phys. 27 (2025) 064504);
the Omega Institute dossier (release 8f61e708e239) and its preregistration issue #14994 (the-omega-institute/trureturing).

Findings: FP conjecture non-negativity (Remark 1, Section 5). FY prove it for qubit inputs with any output dimension
(Theorem 4.1) and report that numerical examples suggest failures in higher dimensions without giving one (Section 6).
WPVF report non-negativity in all known two-time examples. The Omega dossier gives the d = 5 Werner-Holevo counterexample
and lists the smallest input dimension as open (input dimensions 3 and 4 not decided by that family).

Searches: arXiv API (pseudo-density with entropy/subadditivity; states over time with entropy; mutual information in time;
dynamical mutual information; all recent papers by Fullwood and by Parzygnat to November 2026); Semantic Scholar citing
papers of FP, FY and WPVF; GitHub issue search of the Omega repository (Fullwood, pseudo-density, subadditivity qutrit);
general web search. No earlier violation with input dimension below five, qubit output or rank-two input was found, and
no earlier statement of the pure-input bound or of the cross-coherence criterion.
